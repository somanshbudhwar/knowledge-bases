## Overview

AI researcher and educator known for making deep learning accessible. Former Director of AI at Tesla (Autopilot) and founding member of OpenAI.

## Mentioned In

- [[../sources/llm-foundations-learning-roadmap|LLM Foundations Roadmap]]: recommended for his "Zero to Hero" course, which covers building a tokenizer and a tiny GPT from scratch. Listed as a Stage 0 prerequisite resource.

## Key Contributions

- **"Zero to Hero" course**: hands-on series building neural networks from scratch in Python/PyTorch
- **nanoGPT**: minimal GPT implementation used as a learning tool (referenced in the roadmap's methodology section)
- Pioneered accessible AI education through YouTube lectures
