## Overview

Ilya Sutskever is a Ukrainian-Canadian computer scientist and one of the most influential figures in modern deep learning. He co-founded OpenAI and served as its Chief Scientist from 2015 until 2024. He is a central proponent of the **scaling hypothesis** — the belief that increasing compute, data, and model size reliably produces more capable AI.

## Educational Background

- Undergraduate degree, Hebrew University of Jerusalem
- PhD under Geoffrey Hinton at the University of Toronto — one of the "Godfathers of Deep Learning"

## Key Technical Contributions

### AlexNet (2012)

As a PhD student under Hinton, Sutskever co-authored the AlexNet paper (with Alex Krizhevsky and Hinton) that won the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC 2012) by a wide margin, demonstrating that deep convolutional neural networks trained on GPUs could dominate the field. This is widely considered the starting gun of the modern deep learning era.

### Sequence to Sequence Learning (Sutskever, Vinyals, Le — 2014)

Introduced the sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) architecture using LSTMs with an encoder-decoder structure. This enabled neural machine translation and directly preceded the Transformer.

### The Scaling Hypothesis

Sutskever was a key advocate inside OpenAI for the belief that scaling compute and data was the primary path to more capable AI. This motivated:
- GPT-1 (2018): early proof of concept
- GPT-2 (2019): surprised the field with fluent text generation
- GPT-3 (2020, 175B parameters): demonstrated few-shot learning
- GPT-4 (2023): frontier multimodal model

The empirical validation of scaling laws (Kaplan et al., which Sutskever oversaw) provided theoretical grounding for this intuition. See [[scaling-laws]].

## Role at OpenAI

- **Co-founder and Chief Scientist** (2015–2024)
- Led the research team through GPT-1 through GPT-4 development
- Oversaw safety research alongside capability development
- Involved in the board drama of November 2023 that temporarily ousted Sam Altman (Sutskever initially voted for the ouster, then reversed course)
- **Left OpenAI** in May 2024

## Post-OpenAI: Safe Superintelligence (SSI)

In June 2024, Sutskever co-founded **Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI)** with former OpenAI researcher Daniel Gross and Daniel Gross. The company's stated mission is to build a safe superintelligence as its sole goal, without the pressure of product timelines. SSI raised over $1 billion in funding within its first year.

## Views and Philosophy

- Strong believer in the scaling hypothesis as the dominant path to AGI.
- Has expressed concern about AI safety as models approach and exceed human-level intelligence.
- Believes the field should take alignment seriously rather than treating it as a secondary concern.
- Notably predicted (circa 2015) that the main remaining challenge for NLP would be "just" scale.
